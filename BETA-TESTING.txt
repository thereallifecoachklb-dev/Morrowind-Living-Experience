MLX 0.9.0 — PUBLIC BETA TESTING

Play your character normally. Do not grind solely to reach a test threshold.
The goal is slower, meaningful progression, including noncombat lifestyles.

Please report:
- Game / MWSE / MGE XE versions, installed MLX version and other relevant mods.
- New character or existing save; race/class and starting location if relevant.
- What happened, what you expected, and exact steps to reproduce it.
- XP before/after, level and pending skill points where applicable.
- Screenshots and MWSE.log from the affected session (remove private details).
- Whether the issue repeats on reloading a separate test save.

Priority checks:
1. First and second complete level-up cycles, including save/reload persistence.
2. Native attributes/health, allocation and ordinary skill colors/ratings.
3. Paid training and skill books, duplicate rewards and unexpected XP sources.
4. Custom skills, optional integrations and characters without those mods.
5. Modded journal completion, exploration and books from other plugins.
6. Balance: how long did a level take, and what was the character doing?
7. MCM, marker visibility and menu scaling at your resolution.

If allocation fails, keep a separate save and attach the log. F9 only opens
existing pending allocations; it does not grant points. Do not use console
commands to compensate before recording the failure.

Individual XP handlers, native advancement, allocation and MCM have been tested
by the developer in one modded environment. A fresh survival character has been
played through the island escape at level one. Broader compatibility and full
successive advancement remain open beta objectives.
